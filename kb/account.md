# Account & Order Management

## Creating an Account
- Register at https://example.com/register with email and password
- Or sign up using Google, Apple, or Facebook
- Account includes order history, saved addresses, and wishlist

## Order Statuses
- Pending: Payment has not been processed yet
- Processing: Payment confirmed, order being prepared
- Shipped: Package is on its way
- Delivered: Package has been delivered
- Cancelled: Order was cancelled
- Refunded: Order was refunded

## Changing or Cancelling an Order
- Orders can be modified within 1 hour of placement
- After 1 hour, the order may already be in processing
- Contact support immediately if you need to cancel
- If order has shipped, you will need to refuse delivery or initiate a return

## Payment Methods Accepted
- Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- PayPal
- Apple Pay, Google Pay
- Shop Pay
- Buy now, pay later with Affirm (on orders over $50)

## Gift Cards
- Available in $25, $50, $100, $200 denominations
- Sent via email as a digital gift card
- No expiration date
- Cannot be reloaded or exchanged for cash
