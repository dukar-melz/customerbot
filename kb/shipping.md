# Shipping Information

## Standard Shipping (Free)
- Delivery time: 5-8 business days
- Free on orders over $25
- Cost: $4.99 for orders under $25

## Express Shipping
- Delivery time: 2-3 business days
- Cost: $12.99
- Available for all products

## Overnight Shipping
- Delivery time: Next business day (order by 2PM EST)
- Cost: $24.99
- Only available for select products

## International Shipping
- Delivery time: 10-20 business days depending on destination
- Cost: Calculated at checkout based on destination and weight
- Customs fees and import duties may apply

## Order Tracking
- Tracking number is emailed once the order ships
- You can track at: https://example.com/track
- Tracking typically updates within 24-48 hours of shipping

## Shipping Restrictions
- We ship to all 50 US states and 30+ countries
- Some products cannot be shipped internationally due to battery regulations
- PO Box delivery available for standard shipping only
