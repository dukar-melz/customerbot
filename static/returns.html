Returns & Refunds

Standard Returns

  • 30-day return window from delivery date
  • Items must be unused and in original packaging
  • Original receipt or order confirmation required
  • Refund processed within 5–7 business days after we receive the item
  • Refund goes back to original payment method

How to Return

  1. Contact our support team or chat with CustomerBot
  2. You will receive a prepaid return shipping label via email
  3. Pack the item securely with all accessories and original packaging
  4. Drop off at any carrier location
  5. Refund issued once item is received and inspected

Return Shipping Costs

  • Free for defective or incorrect items
  • $5.99 restocking fee for change-of-mind returns
  • Customer responsible for return shipping on international orders

Non-Returnable Items

  • Gift cards
  • Digital products & software downloads
  • Personal care items (earbuds, etc.) if seal is broken
  • Items marked "final sale"

Damaged / Defective Items

Report within 48 hours of delivery. We will send a free replacement or issue a full refund including shipping — no restocking fee.

Warranty Claims

  • All products carry a manufacturer warranty (varies by product)
  • Within 30 days: contact us directly
  • After 30 days: contact the manufacturer
  • We can assist with the warranty process within the first year

Have questions? Chat with our bot or contact support.
